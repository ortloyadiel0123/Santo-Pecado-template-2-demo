Our story

Two rooms.
One address.

Santo Pecado started with a simple idea: McAllen didn't need another coffee shop that closed at 3 PM, or another bar that didn't take its espresso seriously. So we built both, under one roof on Auburn Ave.

The name

"Santo Pecado" — Holy Sin

The name is the whole concept in two words. Come in for something virtuous — a cortado, an avocado toast, a quiet morning with a laptop. Come back for something a little more sinful — a shaken cocktail, a plate of Tacos El Pecado, a night that runs until 2 AM. Both are equally us.

We're not a café that added a bar, or a bar that added espresso as an afterthought. Both programs are built and run with the same standard: made from scratch, sourced with intention, served by people who actually want to be here.

How we run each side

Three programs, one standard

House-made syrups and infusions, fresh citrus, and bartenders who shake every cocktail to order.

The space

Designed to work both shifts

Glossy emerald subway tile, a gold-lined palm mural, black-and-brass slat paneling, marble counters, and a fully stocked back bar — the same room reads completely different in morning light than it does under the pendant lights at night.
